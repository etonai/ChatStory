# Some ideas for DC 11 and beyond:

## Setting up prompts so that ChatGPT responds with both story information and meta information.

Example:

Day 1 Beat 0

Relationship Values:
Drake -> Morgan STP 5.2

"Hi there"

Drake pulls up a chair


In that example, the Relationship Values are not part of the story, they are more meta information.
The Day / Beat can kind of be thought of as both.

The Canon document probably doesn't need the intermediate relationship values. The beats may or may not be useful.
I lean towards the beats being kept - they can be edited out of the canon document later.

additionally, the canon document may want to keep track of all of the characters in the scenes.

That way, a system can scan a canon document's header and find out who is in the document, without searching through the entire document.

## Canon Document consolidation and summarizing
We might create a function that will send a canon document to ChatGPT, and ChatGPT can summarize it and essentially make it more compact.

## Canon document highlights
We may create a highlights section (maybe even in MAIN)
if something important happens in a scene, highlight it and and then save it as a highlight.

Highlights will be elevated in canon and in summarized canon documents.

It can even be used to highlight things like just knowing how much you spent on a car.

The highlights section should be editable by the user. For example, maybe the user just wants to put "Honda Accord purchased for $15,000"

Or maybe the highlight will end up in the canon document.

// HIGHLIGHT: Honda Accord purchased for $15,000
it will be up to the user to figure out how to parse that later.
